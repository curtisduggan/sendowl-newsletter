## Stage 3: CAN Positioning Angles (verbatim)

### How to Position CAN

You know your audience best. Lead with what will resonate and how this fits into what they're already trying to do. Use the angles below as a starting point, and feel free to adapt them in a way that works for your audience.

### 1. The "It Takes Time to Make Money" Angle

- It takes time for Creators to reach the point where they are consistently making money.
- In the meantime, most Creators are spending on tools, platforms, and services to get there.
- CAN helps reduce that cost, so you can build without overpaying.
- Across CAN's offers, that can add up to $15K+ in total value for Members.

### 2. The "Spend to Make, But Spend Smarter" Angle

- You do have to spend to make money as a Creator.
- The difference is how much you spend along the way.
- CAN gives you access to better rates and extended trials, so you can spend more efficiently as you grow.

### 3. The "Keep More of What You Earn" Angle

- Most platforms take a cut, this is one way to keep more of what you earn.
- Many monetization platforms take a significant percentage of your earnings.
- Increasing what you keep is one of the fastest ways to grow your income.
- CAN creates opportunities for Creators to improve their margins without doing more work, including revshare opportunities that pay 10% more across 20k+ brands.
- For example, that can equate to an additional $1,000 per $10,000 in affiliate revenue.

### 4. The "Do More With Less" Angle

- Building a Creator business usually means testing tools, platforms, and workflows.
- CAN gives you access to 35+ partner offers, making it easier to explore what works without committing to full price.
- Extended free trials and discounted offers make it easier to experiment without high upfront costs.
- CAN lowers the barrier for Creators to try new things and find what works.

### 5. The "You've Gotta Try Stuff" Angle

- Building a Creator business is a process of trial and error.
- You might launch a course that doesn't sell, test formats that don't stick, or invest time into platforms that don't pay off.
- That's not wasted effort, it's how you find what works.
- The challenge is that trying things costs money along the way.
- CAN makes that process more affordable by giving you access to better rates, discounts, and extended trials across 35+ tools and platforms.
