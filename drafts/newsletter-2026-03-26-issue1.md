## F. Quick Wins

**Price for what it took to learn, not what it takes to download.** A PDF that contains five years of teaching experience isn't worth $5 just because it's a PDF. Price it based on the outcome it delivers and the expertise behind it.

**Start with your smallest viable audience.** Ann Kullberg launched a digital magazine with 200 fan mail addresses and got 50 subscriptions in two weeks. You don't need 10,000 followers. You need 200 people who care.

**Automate delivery before your third sale.** Manual fulfillment works for your first two customers. By the third, you're losing time you should spend creating. Set up [automated delivery](https://www.sendowl.com/sell-digital-products-online) before it becomes a bottleneck.
